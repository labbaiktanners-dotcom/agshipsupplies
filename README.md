# Agshipsupplies

I own a shipchandlers business in uae

·         My main focus is Chinese ships visiting uae waters.

·         I supply provisions, bond, private orders, gadgets and technical stores (engine/deck/cabin/electric)

·         I prefer the website is Chinese friendly if possible

·         Chinese language option

Give you below as samples
https://www.wrist.com/contact/locations-all-branches/dubai (I like the faq section)

Direct Contact & Location: Place my, WhatsApp link, wechat link, and email at the top. Include a clear physical address, your operating ports (Jebel Ali, Khalifa Port, RAK Mina Saqr, Fujairah Port, Khorfakkan port and all other ports in UAE)

Core Product Categories: Clearly list specialties (e.g., Provisions, Bonded Stores, Private items for crew and Deck/Engine Stores, Cabin/Galley, Safety Equipment)

Single-Page Scroll (The Simplest Option): A single-page layout is cost-effective and mobile-friendly. Put your contact info, product categories, and a quote request form in a simple vertical flow.

“One-Stop Shop” Panel Design: Use a three-panel layout on the homepage to separate your business lines (e.g., Provisions, Technical, Bonded Stores).

A clean, functional design that prioritizes quick contact. Use marine-inspired colors (deep blues, white) and ensure my phone number and whatsapp and wechat are clickable links on mobile.

Request for Quote (RFQ): The primary goal of my site is to generate RFQs. Include a simple form requesting Vessel Name, vessel email, Port of Call, ETA, and Requested Items, or a clear note that purchase orders can be sent to a specific email address. 

Disclaimer somewhere suitably (not footnote) Service Coverage: (e.g., “All ports and anchorages in UAE”)*
(Port & Customs charges and supply boat charges if any are excluded)
