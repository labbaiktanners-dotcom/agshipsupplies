# Arabian Gulf Ship Supplies — Single-Page Website

## Goal
Build a fast, Chinese-friendly one-page website that helps vessel crews and purchasing teams immediately contact Arabian Gulf Ship Supplies or submit a complete request for quotation.

## Page structure
1. **Contact-first header**
   - Company name and English / 简体中文 language switch.
   - Click-to-call mobile number, WhatsApp, WeChat ID, email, and Fujairah address visible immediately.
   - Mobile quick-contact bar for Call, WhatsApp, WeChat, and RFQ.

2. **Opening section**
   - Follow the selected “Neon tech grid” direction: deep navy, ocean blue, cyan highlights, crisp operational grid, and restrained motion.
   - Generate a night-time UAE container-terminal image matching the selected concept.
   - Lead with service for Chinese vessels across UAE ports, with direct RFQ and operations-contact actions.
   - Avoid unsupported claims such as response times, years in business, certifications, or 24/7 availability.

3. **One-stop-shop service panels**
   - Three primary panels: Provisions; Technical Stores; Bonded Stores & Private Crew Orders.
   - Clearly include fresh/dry provisions, gadgets, engine, deck, cabin/galley, electrical, safety equipment, toiletries, and crew purchases.

4. **Ports and service coverage**
   - List Jebel Ali, Khalifa Port, RAK Mina Saqr, Fujairah Port, Khorfakkan Port, and all other UAE ports and anchorages.
   - Present the disclaimer as a visible service notice: port and customs charges, plus supply-boat charges where applicable, are excluded.

5. **FAQ**
   - Use the reference site’s clear, scan-friendly question-and-answer format rather than hiding all answers.
   - Cover port/anchorage delivery, Chinese-friendly ordering, provisions, technical stores, bonded/private orders, and what customers should include in an RFQ.

6. **RFQ and direct contact**
   - Form fields: Vessel Name, Vessel Email, Port of Call, ETA, and Requested Items.
   - Validate required fields, email format, and sensible input lengths in the browser.
   - Let users send the prepared request through email or WhatsApp.
   - Provide WeChat as a copyable ID and scannable code; browsers cannot reliably prefill and send a WeChat message.
   - Add a functional WhatsApp QR code and a WeChat contact/ID QR code, both labeled clearly.
   - State that purchase orders can also be emailed directly to sales@agshipsupplies.com.

## Bilingual behavior
- A real English / 简体中文 switch changes navigation, headings, descriptions, form labels, validation messages, FAQ, coverage text, and disclaimers.
- Use typography that renders both Latin and Simplified Chinese clearly.
- Keep company name, contact details, port names, and identifiers unchanged in both languages.
